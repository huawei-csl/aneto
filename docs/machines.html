Calibrations

The calibrations come from the measurement data of the paper's artifact, for five CPUs and three simulator configurations. The paper tested each calibration on its own machine.

The errors are leave-one-out (LOO). Aneto predicts each workload from a regression fitted without it and without the other phases of its benchmark. Each error compares this prediction with the ground-truth fit of the workload at the target latency. Below the unloaded latency of a machine (DDR5 on most of them), these values can differ from Figure 5 of the paper. These values use the true factor, so they differ from the figure by up to 0.4 percentage points.
